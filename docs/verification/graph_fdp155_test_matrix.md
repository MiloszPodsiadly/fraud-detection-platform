## Status vocabulary

- **PASS** means the named test/source guard ran successfully in this working tree.
- **NOT RUN** means evidence is absent; a related test does not establish the unrun scenario.
- **N/A** means the subsystem or behavior is intentionally absent from this phase. It is not a claim of safety for a future implementation.
